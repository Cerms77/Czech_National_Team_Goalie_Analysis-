Read Full Executive Summary

Project Objective

The models below examine Czech goaltenders competing in the NHL and AHL, comparing their individual performance against league-wide and Czech goaltending averages. This is a sample project; statistics will be updated at a later date before the 2027 IIHF World Championship.

Analytical Approach

The models evaluate traditional statistics such as save percentage, goals against average, and total saves, alongside available advanced analytics including Goals Saved Above Expected (GSAx), high-danger scoring opportunities, low-danger goals allowed, and rebound control.

By examining both the quality of scoring chances faced and a goaltender's performance against expectations, the model aims to provide additional context beyond traditional statistics and team defensive performance.

Application to National Team Scouting

The interactive dashboard allows users to search individual goaltenders, review statistical rankings, and compare performances within their respective leagues. The model is designed to be updated with new NHL and AHL data ahead of future international tournaments, including the 2027 IIHF World Championship.

This project is a developmental demonstration intended to help decision-making, but it is not a replacement for professional scouting and video evaluation.

STATISTIC OVERVIEW

Save Percentage (SV%)

Definition

The share of shots on goal that the goalie stops.

Scouting significance

A quick look at shot stopping, but it does not explain how difficult the shots were.

Higher is generally better.

Scouting Example Tomáš Vokoun vs. Dominik Hašek

Imagine Dominik Hašek stops 93 of 100 shots and Tomáš Vokoun stops 91 of 100. Hašek has the higher save percentage. A scout would still compare the difficulty of the shots faced before drawing a conclusion.
